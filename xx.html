Carry More.
Go Further.

This is a cargo bike built around one idea: the good stuff happens off the paved path. Wider tires, a lower centre of gravity and enough capacity for a week's worth of gear mean the gravel climb, the forgotten fire road and the two-day loop stop being someone else's adventure.

Not Another Cargo Bike.

Most cargo bikes are built to move groceries and kids across town, and they do that well — but that was never the brief here. This one is built for washboard gravel, loose forest descents and the kind of trail that only shows up on a paper map: same carrying capacity, an entirely different terrain.

Built For Detours.

The most memorable rides rarely follow the route you planned. A cargo bay built to hold real weight, tires wide enough for rutted double-track and a frame that stays composed once the trail turns rough mean a detour is as easy to follow as a set of directions — and usually the better story of the two.

Some Rides Require Less.
Some Require More.

A short evening spin needs almost nothing. A weekend out of signal range needs water, food, a stove, something warm to sleep in and somewhere secure to strap all of it down — which is exactly the difference this bike is built to carry.

One Night.
Two Wheels.
Endless Routes.

Pack light enough and a single night away stops being a production. Load the rack, pick a direction with more gravel than pavement, and let the evening work itself out — a bend in the river, a forest clearing, a fire road with a view worth the climb.
