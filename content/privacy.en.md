## 4. Newsletter signup

You can sign up for our newsletter on this website. To do so, we collect your email address.

**Double opt-in:** After signing up you will receive an email containing a confirmation link. We only add you to our mailing list once you have confirmed. This ensures that nobody can sign up someone else's address.

**Processor:** We use [Brevo (Sendinblue GmbH, Köpenicker Straße 126, 10179 Berlin, Germany)] to send our newsletter. Brevo processes your data as a processor on our behalf. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent).

**Retention period:** We store your data until you unsubscribe from the newsletter. After unsubscribing we may keep your email address on a suppression list in order to prevent future mailings.

**Withdrawal:** You can withdraw your consent at any time, for example via the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to [privacy@example.com].

## 5. Spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To protect the signup form against automated submissions we use Cloudflare Turnstile, a service provided by [Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA].

Turnstile checks whether the input comes from a human. In doing so, technical data such as your IP address, browser information and interaction behaviour is transmitted to Cloudflare. According to the provider, no tracking cookies are set.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing abuse).

Further information: [https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

## 6. Fonts
